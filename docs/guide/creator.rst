Project creator and academic affiliations
=========================================

Prof. Rafael S. de Souza
------------------------

**Creator and scientific lead of ModelAtlas**

ModelAtlas was conceived and developed under the scientific direction of
**Prof. Rafael S. de Souza**, an astrophysicist and researcher working at
the intersection of statistical inference, machine learning, and astronomy.

Scientific direction
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The project develops reproducible, mathematically interpretable visual
representations of fitted models. Its research directions include
hexagonal self-organizing maps, learned prototype graphs,
symmetry-aware neural-network comparisons, explainable machine learning,
functional geometry, and statistical identification diagnostics.

Code, figures, tests, and documentation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The project is developed with computer-assisted and AI-assisted coding,
plotting, and documentation workflows, subject to scientific review and
validation. The project repository records the contributions and changes.
Computational assistance should not be confused with independent
scientific authorship. Additional human contributors should receive
credit for their actual contributions as they are documented.
