Methods
-------

* Kohonen, T. (1990). *The self-organizing map*. Proceedings of the IEEE
  78(9), 1464–1480. `doi:10.1109/5.58325 <https://doi.org/10.1109/5.58325>`__.
* Fritzke, B. (1995). *A growing neural gas network learns topologies*.
  Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 7, 625–632.
  `Conference proceedings <https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/1994/hash/d56b9fc4b0f1be8871f5e1c40c0067e7-Abstract.html>`__.
* Bishop, C. M., Svensén, M., & Williams, C. K. I. (1998).
  *GTM: The generative topographic mapping*. Neural Computation 10(1), 215–234.
  `doi:10.1162/089976698300017953 <https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/1998/01/bishop-gtm-ncomp-98.pdf>`__.
* Sundararajan, M., Taly, A., & Yan, Q. (2017).
  *Axiomatic attribution for deep networks*. PMLR 70, 3319–3328.
  `PMLR proceedings <https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/sundararajan17a.html>`__.
* Ainsworth, S. K., Hayase, J., & Srinivasa, S. (2023).
  *Git Re-Basin: Merging models modulo permutation symmetries*. ICLR.
  `arXiv:2209.04836 <https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.04836>`__.
* Manski, C. F. (2003). *Partial Identification of Probability Distributions*.
  Springer. `doi:10.1007/b97478 <https://doi.org/10.1007/b97478>`__.
