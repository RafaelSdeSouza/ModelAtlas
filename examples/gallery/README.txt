Astronomical AI, machine learning and statistical diagnostics
===============================================================

Every figure is generated from Python/Matplotlib in the documentation build.
Examples show how astronomers use SOMs, representation learning, neural
explanations, model comparison, and statistical inference.

Real-data demonstrations use SDSS galaxy colors, SEGUE stellar colors,
and historical solar activity. The photo-z partial-identification example
uses explicitly synthetic photometry.

The intended research communities include Rubin/LSST and DESC,
Euclid and other galaxy-survey photo-z teams, Gaia/SEGUE stellar populations,
time-domain astronomy, and explainable ML. This is topical relevance, not a
claim of validation by those survey collaborations.
